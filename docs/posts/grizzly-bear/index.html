Why Grizzly Bears?

The California grizzly bear was declared extinct in 1922 — hunted to elimination within decades of European settlement. Yet it remains on our state flag. I believe restoring the grizzly is not just an ecological question — it is a question of justice, history, and what we owe to the land.
